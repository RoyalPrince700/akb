### **Slide 2: Executive Decision Summary & Strategic Rationale**

* **Main Concept:** Project ADVANCE is a company-wide marketing campaign that addresses 4 growth priorities:


1. SmartEdu subscriptions must become a shared growth agenda.


2. Internal advocacy and sales of slow moving titles through cross and upselling.


3. New accounts, reactivation and retention must be measured.


4. Validated other Business outcome generated.




* **Executive Decision Summary:**
* Slow digital adoption


* Slow-moving titles


* Increase market shares


* Attract new businesses


* *Core Need:* The business needs an operating model where every individual and function contribute to demand, conversion, service and retention.




* **Strategic Rationale (Why Now?):**
* **Growth Beyond Marketing Department:** Growth gaps are enterprise-wide opportunities requiring active participation from every functional unit.


* **Accelerating SmartEdu Adoption:** Digital subscription growth must become a shared responsibility across the organisation.


* **Cashflow:** Targeted push required to convert slow-moving titles and other products into working capital.


* **Breaking Departmental Silos:** Rewarding non-sales support staff for identifying and connecting new business opportunities.

### **Slide 3: The ADVANCED Growth Agenda**

* **01 New Customer Onboarding:** Verified new customers registered and converted to first transaction.

### **Slide 5: Points System**

* **Reward Verified Business Outcomes Table:**
* **New customer onboarded:** Verified registration — **10 Points**

* **First qualifying purchase:** New customer completes transaction — **30 Points**

* **SmartEdu subscription:** Paid new individual / customer subscription — **30 Points**

* **SmartEdu school / institutional subscription:** Paid school or institution subscription — **50 Points**

* **Slow-moving title sales:** Every ₦50k net qualifying sale — **30 Points**

* **New Business Generated:** Generated income from Trifone, Oxygen, Smipay etc — **40 Points**

* **Textbook business ≥ ₦1m:** Verified net transaction — **40 bonus Points**

* **Cross-sell to existing customer:** New product category purchased — **20 Points**



* **Rule:** No points for fictitious customers, unpaid subscriptions, cancelled orders, duplicate accounts, No credit, inflated transactions or policy breaches. Points should post only after validation and quality-control checks.

### **Slide 6: Reward Framework**

* *Principle:* Recognition is designed to reinforce behaviour, not merely pay cash.


* **Monthly:** Top three Individual, Group, and Leader — ₦50k – ₦150k cash, certificate, and commendation letter.


* **Quarterly:** ADVANCED Champion of the Quarter and Group winner — ₦100k – ₦250k + commendation from the MD.


* **Annual:** Growth Champion, Group, and Leader of the Year — ₦100k – ₦500k + award + learning opportunity.


* **Non-cash recognition:** Certificate, commendation letter, and dinner with the MD.

### **Slide 9: Participating Staff: What We Need From You**

* *Role:* Every participant is expected to act as a growth connector: identify opportunities, pass them through the right channel and support conversion without disrupting core duties.


* **5-Step Process:**
1. **Spot:** Identify schools, customers, subscriptions, textbook or subsidiary opportunities.


2. **Submit:** Enter clean lead details through the agreed log/template.


3. **Support:** Work with Group Leaders and relevant business owners to move opportunities forward.


4. **Validate:** Accept that points count only after finance/audit/owner confirmation.


5. **Report:** Share weekly updates, risks and blockers early.




* **Staff Non-negotiables:** Accuracy of lead details • integrity of customer engagement • no credit sales • no inflated transactions • no disruption to core work.



---

### **Slide 10: Group Leaders: Mobilise, Coordinate, Report**

* *Mandate:* Group Leaders are the execution bridge between staff energy and validated business outcomes. (Set pace • Create rhythm • Remove blockers • Keep records clean • Build healthy competition).


* **Mobilisation:** Explain targets, keep team members engaged and assign clear opportunity owners.


* **Coordination:** Connect leads to Sales, SmartEdu, subsidiaries, Finance or Operations as needed.


* **Tracking:** Maintain weekly status, evidence and conversion movement for each opportunity.


* **Escalation:** Raise blockers early and protect the integrity of reporting and point claims.


* **Success Metrics:** Validated revenue • active participation • clean submissions • conversion speed • team points per employee.



---

### **Slide 11: Subsidiaries and HODs: Make Opportunities Convertible**

* *Premise:* Project ADVANCE will only work if business owners respond quickly, validate cleanly and help staff convert credible opportunities.


* **Workflow Chain:** Staff / Group originates opportunity $\rightarrow$ Group Leader coordinates & tracks $\rightarrow$ Subsidiary / HOD responds and supports conversion $\rightarrow$ Validation Team confirms outcome $\rightarrow$ Leaderboard points and recognition.


* **Subsidiaries should provide:**
1. Clear product/service pitch and qualifying criteria


2. Fast follow-up on referred prospects


3. Price, proposal or onboarding guidance


4. Conversion status and revenue confirmation




* **HODs should provide:**
1. Leadership support and participation discipline


2. Reasonable time for huddles and reporting


3. Help with blockers and cross-functional access


4. Line-of-sight between Project ADVANCE and core work




* **Reporting Rhythm:**
* Weekly: Updates + dashboard


* Monthly: Recognition + management report


* Quarterly: ROI review + scale decision




* **Engagement Ask:** Confirm readiness, escalation owners and turnaround expectations before launch.

* **Week 1 (Approval & calibration):** Board/MD approval, point rules, quality gates and reward budget confirmed.

* **Month 4 (Scale decision):** Evaluate ROI, adjust points and embed into performance culture.


* **Success Metrics:** New customers, SmartEdu subscriptions, slow-moving title revenue, new textbook business, active staff participation, new curriculum, group points, and verified revenue.
