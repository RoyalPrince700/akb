Purpose of the SessionGive colleagues across the Parent Body a clear picture of what Oxygen FM 96.9 does and who it serves.   Explain how a radio station operates: content, audience, production, transmission, compliance and commercial activities.   Equip every subsidiary with enough knowledge to confidently introduce and market Oxygen FM to clients, partners and the public.   Build an internal culture of cross-company referrals, collaboration and shared commercial opportunities.   2. Oxygen FM 96.9 at a GlancePositioning: a youth-focused, educational and community-oriented radio platform.   Core promise: INFORM • INSPIRE • IGNITE.   Content approach: useful knowledge, family-oriented programming, youth development, current affairs, entertainment and community engagement.   Commercial opportunity: advertising, sponsorships, programme partnerships, outside broadcasts, branded content and institutional campaigns.   Role within the Group: a media platform that can amplify the products, services and messages of other subsidiaries.   3. Why a Radio Station Matters to the GroupRadio gives the Group a direct, frequent and local connection with audiences.   It can create awareness for products and services across different market segments.   It provides a platform for thought leadership, public education, CSR and stakeholder engagement.   Cross-selling creates value: a client buying one Group service can be introduced to relevant Oxygen FM solutions—and vice versa.   Every staff member becomes a potential brand ambassador and referral point.   4. Who We Are Speaking ToYoung people and students seeking education, information, opportunity and entertainment.   Parents and families interested in practical, credible and community-relevant content.   Professionals, entrepreneurs, institutions and SMEs seeking visibility and audience engagement.   Government, development organisations, NGOs and educational institutions with public-interest campaigns.   Advertisers and sponsors who need repeated local reach and meaningful audience connection.   5. What Happens Inside a Radio Station?Content Planning — deciding what the audience needs and what the station should sound like.   Programme Production — research, scripting, recording, editing and live presentation.   News & Information — gathering, verifying, writing and presenting relevant information.   Scheduling — placing programmes, commercials, promos and station IDs on the broadcast clock.   Technical Operations — studio systems, playout, transmission, internet/streaming and continuity.   Commercial Operations — selling airtime, sponsorships, branded content and promotional opportunities.   Compliance & Quality Control — ensuring broadcasts meet professional and regulatory requirements.   6. Our Programming PhilosophyEvery programme should have a clear audience, purpose and value proposition.   Content should be informative, credible, engaging and relevant to everyday life.   Education is not limited to the classroom: radio can teach through conversation, storytelling, interviews and practical guidance.   Entertainment should complement—not undermine—the station's identity and responsibility.   Programmes should create opportunities for audience participation and measurable engagement.   7. Oxygen FM Content PillarsKnowledge & Education — learning beyond the classroom.   Youth & Family — issues affecting young people, parents and households.   Health & Wellbeing — practical, responsible public-interest information.   Community & Development — local stories, initiatives and social impact.   Business & Entrepreneurship — opportunities, enterprise and financial awareness.   Culture & Values — Nigerian identity, language, creativity and positive social values.   News & Public Affairs — relevant developments presented responsibly.   8. Our Commercial ProductsSpot Advertising — short commercial messages scheduled within the programme clock.   Programme Sponsorship — association of a brand with a specific programme or segment.   Branded Content — client messages integrated into relevant editorial/commercial formats with appropriate disclosure.   Jingles & Promos — campaign and station awareness messages.   Outside Broadcasts & Activations — taking the station to markets, events, institutions and communities.   Interviews / Sponsored Features — structured opportunities for organisations to explain products, services or initiatives.   Digital Amplification — where applicable, extending campaigns through the station's digital channels.   9. How to Sell Oxygen FM — The Simple ConversationSTEP 1: Understand the client's objective — awareness, sales, launch, education, reputation, community reach or event attendance.   STEP 2: Identify the audience — age, location, interests, occupation and listening behaviour.   STEP 3: Match the objective to a product — spot, sponsorship, branded content, OB, interview or campaign package.   STEP 4: Explain the value — reach + frequency + relevance + credibility + engagement.   STEP 5: Capture the lead — client name, contact, need, budget/timing and decision-maker.   STEP 6: Hand over promptly to the appropriate Oxygen FM commercial team for proposal and negotiation.   10. What Makes a Good Sales Lead?A clearly identified organisation or decision-maker.   A product, service, event or campaign that needs publicity.   A defined target audience.   A likely campaign period or event date.   A reason the client may benefit from radio.   A contact person and reliable phone/email details.   Do not promise prices, discounts, airtime or editorial treatment unless authorised.   11. How Other Subsidiaries Can Support Oxygen FMRefer potential advertisers and institutional partners to the station.   Introduce Oxygen FM when clients request publicity, media coverage or public education.   Include the station in relevant Group proposals where appropriate.   Share sector knowledge that can generate programme ideas and commercial leads.   Use Oxygen FM as a Group communication platform where editorial and commercial policies permit.   Protect the station's reputation: referrals should be credible, professional and aligned with the station's values.   12. What Oxygen FM Can Offer Other Group CompaniesAudience access for product and service awareness.   Public education campaigns and thought-leadership platforms.   Launch and event publicity.   Customer education through sponsored programme segments.   CSR and community-impact storytelling.   Cross-promotion and integrated campaigns across available Group platforms.   Media expertise that can strengthen external client proposals.   13. Compliance, Ethics & Brand ProtectionBroadcasting is regulated; content and commercial messages must comply with applicable Nigerian broadcasting requirements.   Advertising and sponsorship arrangements must be properly authorised and documented.   Editorial independence and commercial interests must be handled responsibly.   Claims made in advertisements should be supportable and appropriate for the product or service.   Staff should escalate sensitive, political, legal, health or potentially harmful content to the appropriate management/editorial authority.   Protecting audience trust protects the long-term commercial value of the station.   14.
